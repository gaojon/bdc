## 二、边界情况（名词表示"地区"时应大写）

| 词库单词 | 应改为 | 词性 | 释义 |
|---|---|---|---|
| arctic    | Arctic    | a; n | (noun) the very cold region around the North Pole |
| antarctic | Antarctic | a; n | (adjective) relating to the very cold region around the South Pole |

说明：作形容词表示"极寒的"时习惯用小写 arctic/antarctic；但名词指代地理区域（the Arctic / the Antarctic）时应大写。
